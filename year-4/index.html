Curriculum map / Year 4

Year 4: Machines That Talk

Big question: Do chatbots know what they are saying?

By the end of this unit, pupils can explain that a chatbot builds answers by predicting likely words, why sounding sure is not the same as being right, why a chatbot is not a friend, and how a better question changes the answer.

Before this, in Year 3, pupils studied AI in Everyday Life. Next, in Year 5, pupils move on to Data, Training and Fairness.

Open the Year 4 folder in Google Drive

How this unit builds

Starting point. Pupils bring AI learning from examples and guessing from patterns, people checking, privacy and Keep to Myself, Hint, not answer, being honest, and people choosing the words for AI.

  1. Lesson 1: What comes next?
    Chatbots predict the next word from patterns.
  2. Lesson 2: Is a chatbot a friend?
    A chatbot is not a friend; talk to trusted adults.
  3. Lesson 3: Sure isn't right
    Sure is not right, so Trust it, Test it or Bin it.
  4. Lesson 4: Ask it better
    Specific prompts, asked for a reason.
  5. Lesson 5: How sure is it?
    AI works out what is likely, not certain.
  6. Lesson 6: Chatty's Rulebook IDEAL Challenge
    Write and test Chatty's Rulebook.

Plan ahead. Lesson 2: Tell your DSL about the lesson beforehand.

Assessment. Teams write Unsure, Honest, Get help and Private rules for Chatty, test another team's rulebook with tricky messages and fix what fails. How assessment works
