Chatty's Rulebook

People decide how chatbots behave.

Preparing to teach

Lesson
Lesson 6 of 6 in Year 4: Machines That Talk
Time
60 minutes, with a 45 minute version in the plan
Pupils learn
  • I can use what I know about chatbots to write rules for a children's chatbot.
  • I can explain why each rule matters.
  • I can test a rulebook with tricky messages.
You need
Printed rulebook sheets, message cards, checklists (slides 18 to 20), a large sheet, an atlas
Preparation
About 10 minutes, plus the plan's Teacher subject knowledge.
What to prepare

Read the safeguarding row above. Print slide 18 (one rulebook sheet per team of four), slide 19 (one set of eight tricky message cards per team, cut out) and slide 20 (one rulebook checklist per team). Head a large sheet of paper "Our Class Rulebook for Chatty". Write sentence starters on the board: "Chatty must say...", "Chatty must never ask for...", "When a child is upset, Chatty must...". Have an atlas to hand for message 7.

If your pupils haven't covered this

Teach Lessons 1 to 5 first. This lesson assesses them, so it is not a starting point for the unit.

What this lesson adds

Pupils apply the whole unit independently, which gives the evidence for the tracker.

The signature activity

Teams write the rules a children's chatbot must follow: say when unsure, never pretend to be a friend, point to trusted adults, protect privacy. Then they test each other's rules with tricky messages.

The slides

Teacher notes and answers are in the lesson plan, and printable resources are at the end of the deck.

What to look for

Exit ticket: 1. Write one rule a good children's chatbot must follow, and say why. 2. A chatbot says: "I'm your best friend!" Which rule is it breaking? 3. What should a good chatbot do if a child says they are upset?

Look for pupils who explain a rule using how chatbots work (predicting words, sounding sure, not knowing you) rather than general reasons, and teams who change a rule because a tricky message caught it out.

This evidence feeds the tracker for How AI works; Staying in charge; Safe, fair and honest; Learning Well; Design and create, and the unit ends with the IDEAL Challenge. Answers are in the plan.
