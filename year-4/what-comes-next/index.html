Curriculum map / Year 4: Machines That Talk

Year 4, Lesson 1

What comes next?

Chatbots build answers by predicting likely next words, one at a time, from patterns in text.

IDEAL: InformedILinks: CS, English

Preparing to teach

Lesson
Lesson 1 of 6 in Year 4: Machines That Talk
Time
60 minutes, with a 45 minute version in the plan
Pupils learn
  • I can explain what a chatbot is.
  • I can predict the next word using a pattern.
  • I can explain why a word chain can sound right but make no sense.
You need
Printed story cards, tally sheets, Dice Charts, sentence sheets (slides 17 to 20), dice, whiteboards
Preparation
About 10 minutes, plus the plan's Teacher subject knowledge.
What to prepare

Print slides 17 to 20: one story card, one tally sheet, one Dice Chart and one sentence sheet per group of three or four. Cut out the story cards (four per page) and the Dice Chart cards. Keep the Dice Charts back until each group has finished tallying. One dice per group. Mini whiteboards and pens for everyone. Write "Fish and ..." on the board.

Pupil devices
None needed
Demonstration
None
Prior learning
Builds on learning from earlier years. Before teaching this lesson

Before teaching this lesson

Pupils should already understand

  • AI spots patterns in its examples and uses them to guess.
  • AI guesses better when it learns from lots of different examples.

Routines it uses: IDEAL moments, the AI Loop, Learning Well.

Where this was taught

  • Earlier year Year 1 Lesson 3: The Mystery Rule (slides 5 and 8)
  • Earlier year Year 1 Lesson 2: Teaching Bleep (slides 8 and 11)

Remember When (slide 2) recalls Year 1 Lesson 2, Year 1 Lesson 3, Year 3 Lesson 2, Year 3 Lesson 6. These are retrieval questions: if your class has not had those lessons, swap them for a question about pupils' own experience. That does not replace any prerequisite above.

If your pupils haven't covered this

Check first: Remember When questions 3 and 4 (slide 2) test this. If most pupils answer well, carry on. If not: Retrieve with RW Q3 and Q4; if new, introduce with Year 1 Lesson 3 slide 8 and Year 1 Lesson 2 slide 11 (about 3 minutes).

What this lesson adds

Pupils learn that a chatbot predicts likely next words from patterns, without understanding them.

The signature activity

The Word Chain Machine: pupils tally which words follow which in a class story, then roll dice to generate sentences that sound right but can be nonsense.

The slides

Teacher notes and answers are in the lesson plan, and printable resources are at the end of the deck.

What to look for

Exit ticket: 1. What is a chatbot? 2. A chatbot makes its answers by predicting the next ______. 3. Why can a word chain make a sentence that sounds right but makes no sense?

Look for pupils who explain the next word by pointing to the tally ("dog came after 'the' three times"), and who can say why a sentence is nonsense even though every word follows the pattern.

This evidence feeds the tracker for How AI works, and the unit ends with the IDEAL Challenge. Answers are in the plan.
