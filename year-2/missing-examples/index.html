Curriculum map / Year 2: Using AI Wisely

Year 2, Lesson 2

Missing examples

When examples are missing, AI gets things wrong for some people and things.

ELinks: CS

The signature activity

Build a Bird: groups teach a partner what a bird is from a card pack, then test with a penguin, an ostrich and a kiwi to see whose pack let them down.

Learning intentions

  • I can say what happens when examples are missing.
  • I can find the missing examples in a pack.
  • I can explain why AI should work for everyone.

The slides

Teacher notes and answers sit in the speaker notes of every slide, and printable resources are at the end of the deck.
