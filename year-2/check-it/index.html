Year 2, Lesson 1

Check it!

AI guesses can be wrong, so people check.

IDEAL: AccurateALinks: DL

Preparing to teach

Lesson
Lesson 1 of 6 in Year 2: Using AI Wisely
Time
60 minutes, with a 45 minute version in the plan
Pupils learn
  • I can say that AI guesses can be wrong.
  • I can check a guess and spot a mistake.
  • I can fix a mistake and say how I know.
You need
Check It paddles made on card (slide 17), Bleep's box of six objects, a muffin
Preparation
About 45 to 60 minutes the first time, plus the plan's Teacher subject knowledge.
What to prepare

Print slide 17 on card, one Check It paddle per pupil plus spares: cut out each pair and glue the Right! and Mistake! sides back to back over a lolly stick or strip of card. Keep the paddles for Lesson 6. Fill Bleep's box (any box or bag) with six classroom objects: an orange, a pencil, a glove, a teddy bear, a book and a toy car. Bring a real muffin or cupcake, or a picture of one, for the worked example on slide 8. Have your Bleep puppet, soft toy or box ready. Plain paper and pencils for Own It.

Pupil devices
None needed
Demonstration
None

Before teaching this lesson

Pupils should already understand

  • Some machines make guesses when they cannot be sure, and many of those use AI.
  • The AI Loop: AI learns from examples, spots and guesses, and a person checks.

Routines it uses: Bleep, IDEAL moments, the AI Loop, Learning Well.

Where this was taught

  • Earlier year Year 1 Lesson 1: Does it guess? (slides 6 and 7)
  • Earlier year Year 1 Lesson 3: The Mystery Rule (slide 8)
  • Earlier year Year 1 Lesson 2: Teaching Bleep (slides 7 to 11)

Remember When (slide 2) recalls Year 1 Lesson 1, Year 1 Lesson 2, Year 1 Lesson 3, Year 1 Lesson 6. These are retrieval questions: if your class has not had those lessons, swap them for a question about pupils' own experience. That does not replace any prerequisite above.

If your pupils haven't covered this

Check first: Remember When questions 2 and 3 and 4 (slide 2) test this. If most pupils answer well, carry on. If not: New class: introduce Year 1 Lesson 1 slides 5 to 7 (5 minutes) and Year 1 Lesson 3 slides 6 to 8 (10 minutes); otherwise retrieve slide 2.

What this lesson adds

Pupils learn that AI guesses can be wrong, and practise checking, fixing and saying how they know.

The signature activity

Check It paddles: Bleep labels pictures (a muffin becomes a puppy) and pupils catch and fix the mistakes.

The slides

Teacher notes and answers are in the lesson plan, and printable resources are at the end of the deck.

What to look for

Exit ticket: 1. Can Bleep's guesses be wrong? 2. Who checks Bleep's guesses? 3. Bleep says this cup is a hat. Fix it and say how you know. (Hold up a cup or a picture of one.)

Look for pupils who look before they lift a paddle, who fix a mistake rather than just saying "wrong", and who can say how they know.

This evidence feeds the tracker for How AI works; Staying in charge, and the unit ends with the IDEAL Challenge. Answers are in the plan.
