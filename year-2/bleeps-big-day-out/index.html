Bleep's Big Day Out

We stay in charge of AI: check its guesses, keep things private, think first.

Lesson 6 of 6 in Year 2: Using AI Wisely
Time
60 minutes, with a 45 minute version in the plan
Pupils learn
  • I can use all five IDEAL habits to stay in charge of Bleep.
  • I can make a choice and say why.
  • I can explain what Bleep got wrong and how I know.
You need

Preparation
About 10 minutes, plus the plan's Teacher subject knowledge.
What to prepare

Pupils need their Check It paddles from Lesson 1; slide 16 reprints them if needed. Print slide 15 (postcards to Bleep), one per pupil. Have your Bleep ready, with a little bag or sun hat for the trip if you can. Have the assessment grid below to hand. Read the story script through once so you can tell it with expression.

Pupils should already understand

  • Everything taught in Lessons 1 to 5 of this unit. This is the end of unit assessment: pupils use that learning on their own.

Remember When (slide 2) recalls Year 1 Lesson 4, Year 2 Lesson 3, Year 2 Lesson 4, Year 2 Lesson 5. These are retrieval questions: if your class has not had those lessons, swap them for a question about pupils' own experience. That does not replace any prerequisite above.

If your pupils haven't covered this

Teach Lessons 1 to 5 first. This lesson assesses them, so it is not a starting point for the unit.

What this lesson adds

Pupils apply the whole unit independently, which gives the evidence for the tracker.

The signature activity

Bleep joins a class trip told as a story; at each stop pupils decide how to keep Bleep in check, keep information private and use Bleep on purpose.

The slides

Teacher notes and answers are in the lesson plan, and printable resources are at the end of the deck.

What to look for

Exit ticket: 1. Bleep guesses wrong. What do you do? 2. Bleep offers to read the park map aloud to find the toilets. Good idea or not? Why? 3. Bleep offers to do your work. What do you say?

Look for pupils who explain each decision by linking it to what they learned (missing examples, personal information, doing the hard thinking), rather than just saying yes or no to Bleep.

This evidence feeds the tracker for All five: How AI works; Staying in charge; Safe, fair and honest; Learning Well; Design and create, and the unit ends with the IDEAL Challenge. Answers are in the plan.
