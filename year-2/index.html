Year 2: Using AI Wisely

Big question: How do we stay in charge of AI?

By the end of this unit, pupils can check and fix an AI's guess, explain why missing examples cause mistakes, try first before asking for help, and stop and ask a grown-up before telling a machine anything personal.

Before this, in Year 1, pupils studied Machines That Guess. Next, in Year 3, pupils move on to AI in Everyday Life.

Open the Year 2 folder in Google Drive

How this unit builds

Starting point. Year 1: AI guesses from examples, the AI Loop, up next, AI pictures, people in charge, Robot Rules and the IDEAL routine.

  1. Lesson 1: Check it!
    AI guesses can be wrong; check, fix, say how you know.
  2. Lesson 2: Missing examples
    Missing examples make AI wrong and unfair.
  3. Lesson 3: Try it first
    Try it first; helper or doer.
  4. Lesson 4: Keep it to yourself
    Personal information is private; Stop, Keep it, Tell.
  5. Lesson 5: AI Helpers Hall of Fame
    Real AI helpers guess while people check.
  6. Lesson 6: Bleep's Big Day Out IDEAL Challenge
    All five habits applied on Bleep's trip.

Assessment. In L6 pupils judge five Bleep ideas, one per habit, with paddles and talk, then write a postcard rule. How assessment works

The slides it uses

  • AI is a computer that makes guesses (Year 1 Lesson 1 slides 6, 7, 10 to 12).
  • AI learns from lots of different examples (Year 1 Lesson 2 slides 7, 8, 11).
  • The AI Loop, with a person checking (Year 1 Lesson 3 slides 6 to 8, poster slide 16).

  • Never tell a machine where you live (Year 1 Lesson 6 slide 8), before Year 2 Lesson 4.
  • Up next shows more of the same (Year 1 Lesson 4 slides 7 to 10), before Year 2 Lesson 6.
  • One session of about 40 minutes: Year 1 Lesson 1 slides 5 to 9, Year 1 Lesson 2 slides 7, 8 and 11, Year 1 Lesson 3 slides 6 to 8.
  • A short introduction is not equivalent to a year of Year 1 teaching; expect to retrieve these ideas often.

Lessons in this unit that depend on earlier ones

  • Year 2 Lesson 2 builds on Year 2 Lesson 1's check and mistake language.
  • Year 2 Lesson 4's exit ticket and Year 2 Lesson 6 use the Check It paddles made in Year 2 Lesson 1.
  • Year 2 Lesson 5 needs Year 2 Lesson 1 (who checks) and uses Year 2 Lesson 3's word helper.
  • Year 2 Lesson 6 Stop 1 needs Year 2 Lesson 4; Stop 2 needs Year 2 Lesson 1 and Year 2 Lesson 2; Stop 3 needs Year 2 Lesson 3.
  • Year 2 Lesson 6 Stop 4 needs Year 1 Lesson 4 up next, not revisited in Year 2.

Pupils who have had the earlier units only need the Remember When retrieval in each lesson. More in Using the lessons.
