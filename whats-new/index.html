What's new

What's new

Changes to IDEAL Thinkers, newest first.

If you've made your own copy of a lesson plan, slides or other resource, your copy doesn't update when mine does, so check here and make a fresh copy of anything marked as updated.

Anyone can follow these changes in an RSS reader, such as Feedly: add the What's new RSS feed.

24 October 2026

Updated

  • The home page now explains where IDEAL Thinkers comes from: the IDEAL framework, the Primary Digital Cognition Curriculum and the thinking behind digital cognition.
  • Ages and US grades for every year group, on the home page, the Year groups page and each year page.
  • Alignment now includes the ISTE Standards for Students and Common Sense Education's Digital Citizenship Curriculum, with the lessons for each.

22 October 2026

New

  • Parents and carers page, with an editable letter for each year group.
  • Catch-up decks for Years 2 to 6, for classes starting part way through the curriculum.
  • Staff meeting decks for every unit, with presenter notes.
  • Printables packs: every printable resource for a unit in one file.
  • Glossary of every word taught, with its meaning and picture.
  • Year 7 transition handover for secondary schools.
  • Celebrating success: a pupil passport, certificate and class list for Canva Bulk Create.
  • Contact page, and a form to tell me you are using IDEAL Thinkers.
  • This What's new page and its RSS feed.

Updated

  • Lessons can now be fitted into your curriculum however suits your school, as long as they are taught in order.
  • Licence page now explains how to ask about commercial use and training.
  • Privacy notice now covers the usage form and Canva.
  • Accessibility improvements across the site.
  • The home page now lists everything that comes with IDEAL Thinkers, under What you get.
  • The meaning of deepfake now covers any AI fake made to look real, not only fakes of people, in Year 6 Lessons 1 and 4, the glossary and the Year 6 letter.

Fixed

  • Links on the page not found page now work from any address.

18 October 2026

New

  • Every lesson page now has Preparing to teach, Before teaching this lesson and What to look for.
  • Every year page now has How this unit builds, and a start box for classes new to IDEAL Thinkers.
  • Privacy notice, linked from every page.

Updated

  • Every lesson plan now has a Before teaching this lesson table that matches its lesson page.
  • Each IDEAL Challenge is now described as the end of unit assessment.
  • Using the lessons now gives one approach for classes starting above Year 1.

16 October 2026

Updated

  • All 36 lesson plans and slide decks revised after a full review, with matching I can statements and exit tickets, a 45 minute version in every plan, and teacher notes by slide in the plans.
  • Printable cards in Years 1 and 2 now have pictures, and pupil card text is larger across the decks.
  • The Year 5 Lesson 2 training and test cards now have drawings of each creature.
  • The road map and posters rebuilt to match the revised lessons.
  • Home page reorganised, starting with At a glance.

Fixed

  • Garbled characters in six lesson files.

14 October 2026

New

  • IDEAL Thinkers launched: 36 lessons from Year 1 to Year 6, each with a lesson plan and slides.
  • The road map and A4 posters.
