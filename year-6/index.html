Year 6: AI, Society and the Future

Big question: Who decides how AI is used?

By the end of this unit, pupils can use a checking routine and trusted sources to decide whether to trust or share a picture, voice or video, explain how AI can magnify unfairness, weigh the costs of AI against its benefits, say who must answer for an AI decision, and design AI for a real problem.

Before this, in Year 5, pupils studied Data, Training and Fairness.

Open the Year 6 folder in Google Drive

How this unit builds

Starting point. Models learn from training data and are tested on unseen data (Y5L2, Y5L6). Bias and balanced data (Y5L4). Consent and permission (Y5L3). Plan, use, prove and STAIR (Y5L5). Rules versus machine learning (Y5L1). Check against evidence and trusted sources (Y4L3).

  1. Lesson 1: Real or generated?
    Generated media, Pause, Look, Ask, Check, and rules for real people.
  2. Lesson 2: Who's missing from the picture?
    AI images repeat stereotypes from their data.
  3. Lesson 3: AI at work
    Automation and what stays human at work.
  4. Lesson 4: Is it worth it?
    AI's planet cost and when it is worth it.
  5. Lesson 5: Who's responsible?
    Who is accountable when a system fails.
  6. Lesson 6: AI for Good Design Studio IDEAL Challenge
    Design, audit and pitch an AI helper.

Plan ahead. Lesson 6: Two sessions; brief the panel before Session 2.

Assessment. In Lesson 6 teams design an AI helper on the AI Loop, audit it letter by letter, pitch it to a panel and write an AI learning promise. How assessment works

Starting Year 6 with pupils new to IDEAL Thinkers

  • Before Lesson 1: AI Loop with Year 1 Lesson 3 slide 8 and trusted sources with Year 4 Lesson 3 slide 10 (5 minutes).
  • Before Lesson 1: consent with Year 5 Lesson 3 slide 5 (2 minutes).
  • Before Lesson 2: training data and bias with Year 5 Lesson 2 slide 5 and Year 5 Lesson 4 slides 5 and 10 (6 minutes).
  • Before Lesson 3: Year 5 Lesson 1 slide 6 and Year 5 Lesson 5 slide 6 (5 minutes).
  • Use Year 5 Lesson 6 slides 9 to 11 for Lesson 1's Remember When question 1 (3 minutes).
  • Honest note: a few slides cannot replace Year 5, so Lesson 6 will be harder for these pupils.

Lessons in this unit that depend on earlier ones

  • Year 6 Lesson 3 Remember When uses Year 6 Lesson 1 and Year 6 Lesson 2.
  • Year 6 Lesson 4 uses Year 6 Lesson 3 (automation) in Remember When.
  • Year 6 Lesson 5 needs Year 6 Lesson 3 (decisions someone must answer for).
  • Year 6 Lesson 6 needs Year 6 Lesson 4 (energy cost) and Year 6 Lesson 5 (accountability), and uses Year 6 Lesson 1 and Year 6 Lesson 2 in the audit.

Pupils who have had the earlier units only need the Remember When retrieval in each lesson. More in Using the lessons.
