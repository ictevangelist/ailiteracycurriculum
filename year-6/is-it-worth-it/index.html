Year 6, Lesson 4

Is it worth it?

AI uses energy, water and materials, and can also help the planet, so we decide when it is worth the cost.

IDEAL: Deliberate, EthicalDELinks: science, geography

Preparing to teach

Lesson
Lesson 4 of 6 in Year 6: AI, Society and the Future
Time
60 minutes, with a 45 minute version in the plan
Pupils learn
  • I can explain what a data centre is and what AI costs the planet.
  • I can decide when using AI is worth its cost.
  • I can describe real ways AI is helping the planet.
You need
Printed job cards, planning sheets, Planet Promise cards (slides 16 to 18), Power Tokens
Preparation
About 10 minutes, plus the plan's Teacher subject knowledge.
What to prepare

Print slide 16 (job cards), one set per team of four, and cut them up. Print slide 17 (planning sheet), one per team, and slide 18 (Planet Promise cards), one per pupil. Prepare 10 Power Tokens per team: counters, cubes or paper squares. No devices are needed.

Pupil devices
None needed
Demonstration
None
Prior learning
Builds on learning from earlier years. Before teaching this lesson

Before teaching this lesson

Pupils should already understand

  • Doing a task yourself is often how you learn, so AI is not always the best help.

Routines it uses: IDEAL moments, STAIR, Learning Well.

Where this was taught

  • Earlier year Year 2 Lesson 3: Try it first (slides 4 and 12)
  • Earlier year Year 5 Lesson 5: Climb the STAIR (slide 6)

Remember When (slide 2) recalls Year 5 Lesson 5, Year 5 Lesson 6, Year 6 Lesson 1, Year 6 Lesson 3. These are retrieval questions: if your class has not had those lessons, swap them for a question about pupils' own experience. That does not replace any prerequisite above.

If your pupils haven't covered this

Check first: Remember When question 4 (slide 2) tests this. If most pupils answer well, carry on. If not: Retrieve or introduce: show Year 5 Lesson 5 slide 6 and say if AI does the thinking you do not learn (2 minutes).

What this lesson adds

Pupils learn that AI uses energy, water and materials and decide, goal first, when it is worth the cost.

The signature activity

Power Tokens: teams plan a class project on a limited energy budget, deciding where AI is worth its cost, and write a Planet Promise.

The slides

Teacher notes and answers are in the lesson plan, and printable resources are at the end of the deck.

What to look for

Exit ticket: 1. A friend wants AI to make a video birthday card when a drawn card would do. Is it worth it? Why? 2. Name two things AI uses that cost the planet. 3. Give one real example of AI helping the planet.

Look for pupils who start from the goal before deciding whether to use AI, who notice that video costs far more, and who can name a benefit as well as a cost.

This evidence feeds the tracker for How AI works; Safe, fair and honest, and the unit ends with the IDEAL Challenge. Answers are in the plan.
