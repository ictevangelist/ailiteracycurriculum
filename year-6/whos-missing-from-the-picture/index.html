Curriculum map / Year 6: AI, Society and the Future

Year 6, Lesson 2

Who's missing from the picture?

AI can repeat and magnify the patterns, and the unfairness, in its data.

EALinks: DC

The signature activity

Draw, Tally, Compare: pupils quickly draw "a scientist", tally their own results, compare them with what image generators tend to produce, then fix the recipe.

Learning intentions

  • I can use data to compare pictures of people.
  • I can explain how AI can repeat and magnify unfair patterns.
  • I can suggest fair ways to fix the recipe.

The slides

Teacher notes and answers sit in the speaker notes of every slide, and printable resources are at the end of the deck.
