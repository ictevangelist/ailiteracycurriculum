Designing AI that helps people.

IDEALLinks: All strands

The signature activity

Teams scope a real problem in school or the community, design an AI helper using the AI Loop, run an IDEAL audit, pitch to a panel, and each pupil writes an AI learning promise for secondary school.

Learning intentions

  • I can choose a real problem and decide whether AI is the right tool.
  • I can design an AI helper on the AI Loop and test it with an IDEAL audit.
  • I can pitch our design, and write my own AI learning promise.

The slides

Teacher notes and answers sit in the speaker notes of every slide, and printable resources are at the end of the deck.
