IDEAL ThinkersAn AI literacy and fluency curriculum

60 minute lessons, with a 45 minute version

Every lesson has the same shape

  1. 1Remember When5 to 7 minFour quick retrieval questions from last lesson, earlier this year and a previous year.
  2. 2Link It5 minA hook that connects to what children already know from their own lives.
  3. 3Learn It10 to 12 minExplicit teaching of one big idea and its vocabulary, with a worked example and a teacher think aloud.
  4. 4Try It15 to 20 minThe signature activity: collaborative, practical and memorable, usually unplugged.
  5. 5Own It10 minPupils apply the idea independently or in pairs, usually by creating something.
  6. 6IDEAL Check3 minWhich IDEAL letter did we practise, and what does it look like in real life?
  7. 7Exit Ticket3 to 5 minThree short questions to check understanding and shape the next lesson.
Retrieve, never re-teachSlides build on clickUnplugged firstNo pupil accounts

Lesson 6 each year is the IDEAL Challenge and the end of unit assessment.
