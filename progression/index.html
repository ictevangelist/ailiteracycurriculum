How the learning builds

Five threads of progression across KS1, Lower KS2 and Upper KS2, and where each AI Literacy outcome is taught.

Alignment

National Curriculum for computing (England): using technology safely, respectfully and responsibly; keeping personal information private; recognising common uses of information technology beyond school (KS1); understanding how search results are selected and ranked, and being discerning in evaluating digital content (KS2).

SEAME: lessons cover all four levels of the model: social and ethical, applications, models and, through unplugged activities, engines.

The new computing curriculum: BCS is drafting a revised National Curriculum for Computing for first teaching from September 2028, with foundational AI concepts included. This curriculum will be mapped against the draft once it is published.

Wider curriculum: RSHE, science, maths, English, geography and careers links are flagged in each lesson plan.
