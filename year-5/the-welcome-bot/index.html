Curriculum map / Year 5: Data, Training and Fairness

Year 5, Lesson 4

The Welcome Bot

Unbalanced data leads to unfair results.

EALinks: CS, DC

The signature activity

With a budget of twelve voice samples, teams choose who to train a school Welcome Bot on, then test it on visitors to see who it fails.

Learning intentions

  • I can explain how unbalanced training data can make AI unfair.
  • I can choose training data and test who the AI fails.
  • I can explain what balanced data is and why it matters.

The slides

Teacher notes and answers sit in the speaker notes of every slide, and printable resources are at the end of the deck.
