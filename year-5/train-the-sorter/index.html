Curriculum map / Year 5: Data, Training and Fairness

Year 5, Lesson 2

Train the sorter

AI is trained on one set of data and tested on another.

LALinks: CS, maths

The signature activity

Zorbs and Glims: teams write rules from labelled creature cards, test them on unseen cards and score their accuracy out of ten.

Learning intentions

  • I can explain the difference between training data and test data.
  • I can write a sorting rule from examples and test it fairly.
  • I can work out accuracy and use it to decide how far to trust a model.

The slides

Teacher notes and answers sit in the speaker notes of every slide, and printable resources are at the end of the deck.
