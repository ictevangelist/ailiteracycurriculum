Curriculum map / Year 5: Data, Training and Fairness

Year 5, Lesson 6 IDEAL Challenge

Train a class model

Building, testing and improving a real AI model.

IDEALLinks: CS, science

Teacher demonstration. Where a demonstration helps, the teacher runs it on a school approved tool, previews it first and stays in control. The deck also includes prepared examples, so the lesson does not depend on a live demonstration working on the day. No pupil accounts. More on safety

The signature activity

Teacher-led, with no pupil accounts: the class trains an image model to sort recycling, tests it fairly, finds where it fails and improves the data.

Learning intentions

  • I can plan a fair, balanced dataset.
  • I can train and test a model fairly, and work out its accuracy.
  • I can find where our model falls short, improve the data and explain what we did.

The slides
