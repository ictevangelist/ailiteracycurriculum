Curriculum map / Year 5: Data, Training and Fairness

Year 5, Lesson 3

Whose is it anyway?

Training data comes from people's work and lives, so permission matters.

IDEAL: EthicalELinks: DC

The signature activity

The Robot Art Company collects pupils' drawings without asking; the class unpicks how that felt and writes a Permission Charter.

Learning intentions

  • I can explain where AI training data comes from.
  • I can explain why permission and consent matter.
  • I can suggest a fair rule for using people's work and explain why it is fair.

The slides
