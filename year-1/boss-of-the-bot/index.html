Boss of the Bot

People make AI, and people should stay in charge of it.

Preparing to teach

Lesson
Lesson 6 of 6 in Year 1: Machines That Guess
Time
60 minutes, with a 45 minute version in the plan
Pupils learn
  • I can say who is in charge of AI.
  • I can choose good jobs for a robot helper.
  • I can give a reason for one of our Robot Rules.
You need
Bleep, printed job cards and column signs (slides 14 and 16), a pot of pencils
Preparation
About 15 minutes, plus the plan's Teacher subject knowledge.
What to prepare

Bleep (the same as earlier lessons). Job cards: print slide 14 and cut out the nine cards; add a picture to each if you can. Column signs: print slide 16 and stick the three signs on the board or beside three hoops on the carpet. Robot Rules poster: print slide 15 on A3 after the lesson, edited to your class's words. A pot of pencils for the worked example. Own It: paper and crayons for every pupil, and a copy of the success criteria below for your notes.

Before teaching this lesson

Pupils should already understand

  • Everything taught in Lessons 1 to 5 of this unit. This is the end of unit assessment: pupils use that learning on their own.

Routines it uses: IDEAL moments, Bleep, Learning Well.

Where this was taught

  • This unit Year 1 Lesson 1: Does it guess?
  • This unit Year 1 Lesson 2: Teaching Bleep
  • This unit Year 1 Lesson 3: The Mystery Rule
  • This unit Year 1 Lesson 4: Why did it show me that?
  • This unit Year 1 Lesson 5: Picture this

Remember When (slide 2) recalls Year 1 Lesson 2, Year 1 Lesson 3, Year 1 Lesson 4, Year 1 Lesson 5. These are retrieval questions: if your class has not had those lessons, swap them for a question about pupils' own experience. That does not replace any prerequisite above.

If your pupils haven't covered this

Teach Lessons 1 to 5 first. This lesson assesses them, so it is not a starting point for the unit.

What this lesson adds

Pupils apply the whole unit independently, which gives the evidence for the tracker.

The signature activity

Pupils design jobs for a classroom helper robot, decide what only people should do and what we never tell a machine, and write the class Robot Rules.

The slides

Teacher notes and answers are in the lesson plan, and printable resources are at the end of the deck.

What to look for

Exit ticket: 1. Bleep says this pencil is a crayon. Why might Bleep get it wrong, and who checks? 2. Name a job Bleep can help with, and a job only people should do. 3. What do we never tell a machine?

Look for pupils who use words from the whole unit without prompting (guess, examples, check, choose, in charge), who include the person who checks in their drawing, and who can say why a job needs a person.

This evidence feeds the tracker for Staying in charge; Safe, fair and honest; Design and create, and the unit ends with the IDEAL Challenge. Answers are in the plan.
