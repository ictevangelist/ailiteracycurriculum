Curriculum map / Year 1: Machines That Guess

Year 1, Lesson 2

Teaching Bleep

AI learns from lots of examples.

LLinks: CS

The signature activity

Bleep, the class robot puppet, is taught what fruit is. Two examples lead to silly guesses; many varied examples make Bleep better.

Learning intentions

  • I can say what an example is.
  • I can explain why Bleep needs lots of examples.
  • I can pick different examples to teach Bleep.
