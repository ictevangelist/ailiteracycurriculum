Curriculum map / Year 1

Year 1: Machines That Guess

Big question: Can a machine make a guess?

By the end of this unit, pupils can tell a machine that follows steps from one that makes guesses, explain that AI learns from examples, and say who checks its guesses.

Next, in Year 2, pupils move on to Using AI Wisely.

How this unit builds

Starting point. Pupils start the curriculum here, from their everyday experience.

  1. Lesson 1: Does it guess?
    Some machines guess, and those use AI.
  2. Lesson 2: Teaching Bleep
    AI learns from lots of different examples.
  3. Lesson 3: The Mystery Rule
    The AI Loop; a person checks the guess.
  4. Lesson 4: Why did it show me that?
    The loop explains up next, and we choose on purpose.
  5. Lesson 5: Picture this
    AI makes pictures from words people choose, and we say who made it.
  6. Lesson 6: Boss of the Bot IDEAL Challenge
    People stay in charge; jobs, never tell, Robot Rules.

Plan ahead. Lesson 5: Make and check the AI pictures, and preview the live demonstration.

Assessment. In L6 pupils sort robot jobs, write Robot Rules and draw Bleep with a checker, assessed against all five progression strands. How assessment works

Starting Year 1 with pupils new to IDEAL Thinkers

Year 1 is the start of the curriculum: no introduction is needed.

Lessons in this unit that depend on earlier ones

  • Year 1 Lesson 3 needs Year 1 Lesson 2: the Learn step is learning from examples.
  • Year 1 Lesson 4 needs Year 1 Lesson 3's AI Loop and Year 1 Lesson 1's video app as a guessing machine.
  • Year 1 Lesson 5 needs Year 1 Lesson 3's person-checks idea to check the AI picture.
  • Year 1 Lesson 6 needs Year 1 Lesson 2, Year 1 Lesson 3, Year 1 Lesson 4 and Year 1 Lesson 5, and Year 1 Lesson 1's five habits for the Robot Rules.

Pupils who have had the earlier units only need the Remember When retrieval in each lesson. More in Using the lessons.
