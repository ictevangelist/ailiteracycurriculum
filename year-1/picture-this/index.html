Lesson
Lesson 5 of 6 in Year 1: Machines That Guess
Time
60 minutes, with a 45 minute version in the plan
Pupils learn
  • I can describe a picture using words.
  • I can say who made a picture.
  • I can choose my words on purpose.
You need
Prepared AI pictures on slide 10, Bleep, printed describe cards (slide 16), paper, crayons
Preparation
About 20 minutes, plus the plan's Teacher subject knowledge. Plan ahead: Make and check the AI pictures, and preview the live demonstration.
What to prepare

Prepared pictures (essential): on your school approved AI picture tool, type exactly the two descriptions below, check every picture produced, and paste one of each onto slide 10 in place of the "Teacher: paste..." lines. Preview the live demonstration: try one practice description and find your projector's freeze or hide button. Bleep (the same as earlier lessons). Describe cards: print slide 16 and stick the four cards on the board. For every pupil: paper and crayons. Unplugged version: board pens, or two large sheets of paper if you could not make prepared pictures.

Pupil devices
None needed
Demonstration
Optional: the teacher runs it on a school approved tool, or uses the prepared results in the plan

Before teaching this lesson

Pupils should already understand

  • AI makes guesses from its examples, so an AI picture is its guess at our words.
  • A person checks what AI produces, because AI cannot check itself.

Routines it uses: IDEAL moments, Bleep, Learning Well.

Where this was taught

  • This unit Year 1 Lesson 1: Does it guess? (slides 6 and 7)
  • This unit Year 1 Lesson 3: The Mystery Rule (slide 8)

Remember When (slide 2) recalls Year 1 Lesson 1, Year 1 Lesson 3, Year 1 Lesson 4. These are retrieval questions: if your class has not had those lessons, swap them for a question about pupils' own experience. That does not replace any prerequisite above.

If your pupils haven't covered this

Check first: Remember When questions 3 and 4 (slide 2) test this. If most pupils answer well, carry on. If not: Introduce: Year 1 Lesson 1 slide 6 (AI guesses) and Year 1 Lesson 3 slide 8 (a person checks), about 5 minutes together.

What this lesson adds

Pupils learn that AI can make pictures from words that people choose, and that we say who made each picture.

The signature activity

The class designs a picture in words, the teacher creates it with a school approved tool, and pupils compare it with their own drawings: who made which?

The slides

Teacher notes and answers are in the lesson plan, and printable resources are at the end of the deck.

What to look for

Exit ticket: 1. We asked for "a dog" and got a small brown dog. We wanted a big spotty dog. What do we change? 2. Who made the AI picture? Who chose the words? 3. What should we say when we show an AI picture?

Look for pupils who use more than one describing word on purpose, who check the AI picture against the words, and who say without prompting that the tool made the picture but people chose the words.

This evidence feeds the tracker for How AI works; Staying in charge; Safe, fair and honest, and the unit ends with the IDEAL Challenge. Answers are in the plan.
