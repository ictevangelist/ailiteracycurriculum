Year 1, Lesson 3

The Mystery Rule

AI spots patterns, then guesses. Meet the AI Loop.

IDEAL: Informed, AccurateIALinks: CS, maths

Preparing to teach

Lesson
Lesson 3 of 6 in Year 1: Machines That Guess
Time
60 minutes, with a 45 minute version in the plan
Pupils learn
  • I can spot a pattern and a rule.
  • I can say the four steps of the AI Loop.
  • I can make a secret rule for my partner.
You need
Two hoops, two bags of objects or pictures, object trays, printed slides 14 to 16
Preparation
About 30 minutes the first time, plus the plan's Teacher subject knowledge.
What to prepare

Hoops: two PE hoops or sorting rings on the carpet, or the hoop mats on slide 14. Round 1 bag, in this order: a toy car, a book, a toy bus, an apple, a bike (a toy or a picture), a cup. Round 2: toy animals or pictures of a bird, a dog, a butterfly, a fish, a bee and a cat. Own It: one tray of mixed classroom objects per pair (crayons, blocks, shapes, counters, small toys) and two hoop mats or two sheets of paper per pair. Printables: print slide 14 (one per pair, ideally A3), print and cut out slide 15 (secret rule cards for pupils who need them), and print slide 16 in colour for the working wall.

Pupil devices
None needed
Demonstration
None

Before teaching this lesson

Pupils should already understand

  • AI learns from lots of different examples, so too few or similar examples cause silly guesses.
  • Some machines make guesses when they cannot be sure, and many of those use AI.

Routines it uses: IDEAL moments, Bleep, Learning Well.

Where this was taught

  • This unit Year 1 Lesson 2: Teaching Bleep (slides 7 to 11)
  • This unit Year 1 Lesson 1: Does it guess? (slides 6 and 7)

Remember When (slide 2) recalls Year 1 Lesson 1, Year 1 Lesson 2. These are retrieval questions: if your class has not had those lessons, swap them for a question about pupils' own experience. That does not replace any prerequisite above.

If your pupils haven't covered this

Check first: Remember When questions 1 and 2 and 3 and 4 (slide 2) test this. If most pupils answer well, carry on. If not: Introduce: use Year 1 Lesson 2 slides 7, 8 and 11 (two red apples, silly guesses, takeaway), about 6 minutes.

What this lesson adds

Pupils learn the AI Loop: AI learns from examples, spots a pattern and guesses, and a person checks.

The signature activity

The teacher sorts objects into two hoops using a secret rule; the class learns, spots, guesses and checks, then pupils invent rules for each other.

The slides

Teacher notes and answers are in the lesson plan, and printable resources are at the end of the deck.

What to look for

Exit ticket: 1. A spoon, a key and a coin go in Hoop 1. A sock goes in Hoop 2. Where does a paper clip go? What is the rule? 2. Say the AI Loop. Do the actions! 3. Who does the Check?

Look for pupils who say how they spotted the rule ("they all had wheels"), who can name which loop step they are doing during Own It, and who say "a person" without hesitation for the Check.

This evidence feeds the tracker for How AI works; Staying in charge, and the unit ends with the IDEAL Challenge. Answers are in the plan.
