Why did it show me that?

Some AI chooses what we see next, and we can choose on purpose.

IDEAL: Informed, DeliberateIDLinks: DC

Preparing to teach

Lesson
Lesson 4 of 6 in Year 1: Machines That Guess
Time
60 minutes, with a 45 minute version in the plan
Pupils learn
  • I can say what "up next" means.
  • I can explain how Bleep chooses what to show me.
  • I can choose on purpose what I watch.
You need
Bleep, printed video cards with pictures and shelf signs (slides 15 and 16), paper, crayons
Preparation
About 15 minutes, plus the plan's Teacher subject knowledge.
What to prepare

Bleep (the same as earlier lessons). Video cards: print slide 15 and cut out the 20 cards (five each of dinosaurs, cats, football and space); stick a picture on each card, so pupils who cannot read the words or tell the colours apart still know each kind. Bleep's shelf: print slide 16; set out the four Up next signs in a row (a pocket chart, the whiteboard ledge or four chairs), the Now watching sign beside them, and the Video box sign on a box or tray holding the spare cards. Start with one card of each kind on the shelf. Own It: one sheet of paper per pupil, folded into four, and crayons.

Pupil devices
None needed
Demonstration
None

Before teaching this lesson

Pupils should already understand

  • The AI Loop: AI learns, spots and guesses, and a person checks.
  • A video app that picks the next cartoon is a machine that makes a guess.

Routines it uses: IDEAL moments, Bleep, the AI Loop, Learning Well.

Where this was taught

  • This unit Year 1 Lesson 3: The Mystery Rule (slide 8)
  • This unit Year 1 Lesson 1: Does it guess? (slide 11)

Remember When (slide 2) recalls Year 1 Lesson 1, Year 1 Lesson 2, Year 1 Lesson 3. These are retrieval questions: if your class has not had those lessons, swap them for a question about pupils' own experience. That does not replace any prerequisite above.

If your pupils haven't covered this

Check first: Remember When questions 1 and 2 and 4 (slide 2) test this. If most pupils answer well, carry on. If not: Introduce: run Year 1 Lesson 3 slides 6 to 8 (Mystery Rule Round 1, then the AI Loop with actions), about 10 minutes.

What this lesson adds

Pupils learn that some AI chooses what we see next from what we picked, and that we can choose on purpose.

The signature activity

Bleep's Video Shelf: the cards children pick change what Bleep puts on the "up next" shelf, until the shelf only shows one thing.

The slides

Teacher notes and answers are in the lesson plan, and printable resources are at the end of the deck.

What to look for

Exit ticket: 1. Mia's tablet only shows her football videos now. Why? What could she do? 2. Why did Bleep's shelf fill up with one thing? 3. How can you choose on purpose?

Look for pupils who explain the shelf using what was picked before ("because we kept choosing dinosaurs"), who connect Bleep TV to the AI Loop, and whose own shelf shows a deliberate mix.

This evidence feeds the tracker for How AI works; Staying in charge, and the unit ends with the IDEAL Challenge. Answers are in the plan.
