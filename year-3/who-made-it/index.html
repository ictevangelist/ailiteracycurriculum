Year 3, Lesson 5

Who made it?

Being honest about when AI has helped.

IDEAL: EthicalELinks: DC, DL

Preparing to teach

Lesson
Lesson 5 of 6 in Year 3: AI in Everyday Life
Time
60 minutes, with a 45 minute version in the plan
Pupils learn
  • I can explain why it is honest to say when AI has helped.
  • I can decide how much AI helped with a piece of work.
  • I can design a "Made with AI" label that tells the truth.
You need
Bleep, printed letter sets (slide 17) and blank label sheets (slide 18)
Preparation
About 10 minutes, plus the plan's Teacher subject knowledge.
What to prepare

Print slide 17 (Bleep's four letters), one set per group of three or four, and cut them out. Print slide 18 (blank labels), one sheet per pair. Have Bleep ready. If you have a real example of something you made with a little AI help (such as a poster with an AI picture), bring it for slide 7; otherwise use the example on the slide. No devices are needed.

Pupil devices
None needed
Demonstration
None

Before teaching this lesson

Pupils should already understand

  • People choose the words, but the AI tool makes the picture, so we say who made it.
  • When a doer does the work for you, the work is not yours.

Routines it uses: Bleep, IDEAL moments, Learning Well.

Where this was taught

  • Earlier year Year 1 Lesson 5: Picture this (slides 8, 11 and 12)
  • Earlier year Year 2 Lesson 3: Try it first (slide 12)

Remember When (slide 2) recalls Year 1 Lesson 5, Year 2 Lesson 3, Year 3 Lesson 3, Year 3 Lesson 4. These are retrieval questions: if your class has not had those lessons, swap them for a question about pupils' own experience. That does not replace any prerequisite above.

If your pupils haven't covered this

Check first: Remember When questions 3 and 4 (slide 2) test this. If most pupils answer well, carry on. If not: Retrieve with RW Q3 and Q4; if new, use Year 1 Lesson 5 slides 11 to 12 and Year 2 Lesson 3 slide 12 (about 5 minutes).

What this lesson adds

Pupils learn to judge how much AI helped and to label it honestly.

The signature activity

Dear Bleep: Bleep writes for advice on tricky situations about schoolwork, art and stories; pupils reply and design a "Made with AI" label.

The slides

Teacher notes and answers are in the lesson plan, and printable resources are at the end of the deck.

What to look for

Exit ticket: 1. What does honest mean when AI has helped you? 2. AI gave you the idea for your story ending. Write the label you would add. 3. True or false? If AI only helped a little, you do not need to say.

Look for pupils who separate what AI did from what they did, and who can be honest about a friend in a way that is also kind.

This evidence feeds the tracker for Safe, fair and honest; Staying in charge, and the unit ends with the IDEAL Challenge. Answers are in the plan.
