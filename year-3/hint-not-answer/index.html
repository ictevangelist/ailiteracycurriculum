Curriculum map / Year 3: AI in Everyday Life

Year 3, Lesson 4

Hint, not answer

The best help makes you think.

LDLinks: DL

The signature activity

Helper Cards: pupils rank different kinds of help from "thinks for me" to "makes me think", then write hint questions for a partner.

Learning intentions

  • I can explain the difference between a hint and an answer.
  • I can sort help from "thinks for me" to "makes me think".
  • I can write hints that help a partner think.

The slides

Teacher notes and answers sit in the speaker notes of every slide, and printable resources are at the end of the deck.

Time60 minutes, or 45 by trimming Try It

DevicesUnplugged first: no pupil devices needed
