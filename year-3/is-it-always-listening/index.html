Year 3, Lesson 2

Is it always listening?

Voice assistants listen for a wake word, then record and send what we say.

IDEAL: Informed, EthicalIELinks: DC

Preparing to teach

Lesson
Lesson 2 of 6 in Year 3: AI in Everyday Life
Time
60 minutes, with a 45 minute version in the plan
Pupils learn
  • I can explain how a voice assistant listens for its wake word.
  • I can describe what happens to what it hears after it wakes up.
  • I can decide what should stay private, for me and for other people.
Preparation
About 10 minutes, plus the plan's Teacher subject knowledge.
What to prepare

Read the Wake Word script below and practise reading it at a natural speed. Print slide 16, one recording sheet per pair, and slide 17, one sign template per pair. No devices are needed. Do not bring a real smart speaker into class, and be aware that saying a wake word aloud on slide 5 may wake a real device nearby.

Pupil devices
None needed
Demonstration
None
Safeguarding

Before teaching this lesson

Pupils should already understand

  • Data is information a computer collects and uses.
  • Personal information such as an address is private and kept to ourselves.
  • AI makes guesses, and its guesses can be wrong.

Routines it uses: IDEAL moments, the AI Loop, Learning Well.

Where this was taught

  • This unit Year 3 Lesson 1: What's in your feed? (slide 5)
  • Year 2 Year 2 Lesson 4: Keep it to yourself (slides 5 and 7)
  • Year 2 Year 2 Lesson 1: Check it! (slide 7)

Remember When (slide 2) recalls Year 1 Lesson 1, Year 2 Lesson 4, Year 3 Lesson 1. If your class has not had these, ask about pupils' own experience instead.

If your pupils haven't covered this

Retrieve with RW Q1 to Q3; if new, show Year 3 Lesson 1 slide 5 and Year 2 Lesson 4 slide 7 (about 3 minutes).

What this lesson adds

Pupils learn what a voice assistant records and sends, and that privacy protects other people too.

The signature activity

Wake Word: pupils play a voice assistant listening for its name, discover what it hears along the way, and decide what should stay private.

The slides

Teacher notes and answers are in the lesson plan, and printable resources are at the end of the deck.

What to look for

Exit ticket: 1. What is a wake word? 2. Gran's friend visits and the smart speaker is on. What should you tell her, and why? 3. Give one way a grown up can protect privacy around a voice assistant.

Look for pupils who can say what happens before and after the wake word, and who think about other people's privacy as well as their own.

This evidence feeds the tracker for How AI works; Safe, fair and honest, and the unit ends with the IDEAL Challenge. Answers are in the plan.
