Year 3, Lesson 1

What's in your feed?

Apps learn from what we do, then choose what we see.

IDEAL: Informed, DeliberateIDLinks: DC

Preparing to teach

Lesson
Lesson 1 of 6 in Year 3: AI in Everyday Life
Time
60 minutes, with a 45 minute version in the plan
Pupils learn
  • I can explain what a feed is and how an app learns what to show me.
  • I can spot the data an app collects when I watch, like or skip.
  • I can choose with a reason, instead of letting the feed choose for me.
You need
Printed video cards (slides 17 and 18), tally sheets (slide 19), job cards (slide 16)
Preparation
About 15 minutes, plus the plan's Teacher subject knowledge.
What to prepare

Print slides 17 and 18 once and cut out the 36 video cards (six of each colour); this is the class Feed Machine, and it can be kept for Lesson 6. Print slide 19, one tally sheet per pair. Print slide 16 once and cut out the four job cards. Clear space on the board for a row of six cards, with sticky tack or magnets. No devices are needed.

Pupil devices
None needed

None

Before teaching this lesson

Pupils should already understand

  • Picking one kind of video makes an app show more of it until little else appears.
  • AI learns from examples, spots a pattern and guesses, and a person checks.

Routines it uses: IDEAL moments, the AI Loop, Learning Well.

Where this was taught

  • Earlier year Year 1 Lesson 4: Why did it show me that? (slides 7 to 9)
  • Earlier year Year 1 Lesson 3: The Mystery Rule (slide 8)

Remember When (slide 2) recalls Year 1 Lesson 3, Year 1 Lesson 4, Year 2 Lesson 4, Year 2 Lesson 6. These are retrieval questions: if your class has not had those lessons, swap them for a question about pupils' own experience. That does not replace any prerequisite above.

If your pupils haven't covered this

Check first: Remember When questions 3 and 4 (slide 2) test this. If most pupils answer well, carry on. If not: Retrieve with RW Q3 and Q4; if new, introduce with Year 1 Lesson 4 slides 7 to 8 and Year 1 Lesson 3 slide 8 (about 6 minutes).

What this lesson adds

Pupils see that every watch, like and skip is data a real app uses to recommend more of the same.

The signature activity

The Feed Machine: the class acts as a video app that learns from every like, and watches its feed shrink into a bubble.

The slides

Teacher notes and answers are in the lesson plan, and printable resources are at the end of the deck.

What to look for

Exit ticket: 1. Leo skips every cooking video but watches football to the end. What will his feed show more of, and how does the app know? 2. Name two kinds of data an app collects while you watch. 3. Your feed only shows one kind of video. Give one way to take charge.

Look for pupils who say that skipping and watching are data as well as likes, and who can say who chose the feed: the app, from the data.

This evidence feeds the tracker for How AI works; Staying in charge, and the unit ends with the IDEAL Challenge. Answers are in the plan.
