Curriculum map / Year 3: AI in Everyday Life

Year 3, Lesson 1

What's in your feed?

Apps learn from what we do, then choose what we see.

IDLinks: DC

The signature activity

The Feed Machine: the class acts as a video app that learns from every like, and watches its feed shrink into a bubble.

Learning intentions

  • I can explain what a feed is and how an app learns what to show me.
  • I can spot the data an app collects when I watch, like or skip.
  • I can choose with a reason, instead of letting the feed choose for me.
