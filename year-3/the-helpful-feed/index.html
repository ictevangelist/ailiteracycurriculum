Year 3, Lesson 6 IDEAL Challenge

The Helpful Feed

Designing technology that is good for children.

IDEALLinks: DC, CS

The signature activity

Teams redesign a children's video app: a "why am I seeing this?" button, healthy limits, clear AI labels, no personal data. They pitch their design to the class.

Learning intentions

  • I can use what I know about AI to design a feed that is good for children.
  • I can explain how each part of my design helps children's wellbeing.
  • I can pitch my design and give fair, kind feedback to others.

The slides

Teacher notes and answers sit in the speaker notes of every slide, and printable resources are at the end of the deck.
