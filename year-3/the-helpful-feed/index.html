Year 3, Lesson 6 IDEAL Challenge

The Helpful Feed

Technology for children should explain its choices, label AI and keep data private.

IDEAL: all five habitsIDEALLinks: DC, CS

Preparing to teach

Lesson
Lesson 6 of 6 in Year 3: AI in Everyday Life
Time
60 minutes, with a 45 minute version in the plan
Pupils learn
  • I can use what I know about AI to design a feed that is good for children.
  • I can explain how each part of my design helps children's wellbeing.
  • I can explain how one feature of my design uses what I know about feeds.
You need
Printed design sheets, star and wish cards, checklists (slides 15 to 17), felt tips
Preparation
About 15 minutes, plus the plan's Teacher subject knowledge.
What to prepare

Print slide 15 (design sheet), on A3 if you can, one per team of four. Print slide 16 (star and wish cards), one sheet per team, and cut them out. Print slide 17 (team checklist), one per team. Put felt tips, sticky notes and scrap paper on each table. If you kept them, put out the video cards from Lesson 1 and the "Made with AI" labels from Lesson 5 for teams to use. Plan mixed teams of four in advance. No devices are needed.

Pupil devices
None needed
Demonstration
None

Before teaching this lesson

Pupils should already understand

  • Everything taught in Lessons 1 to 5 of this unit. This is the end of unit assessment: pupils use that learning on their own.

Routines it uses: IDEAL moments, Learning Well, the AI Loop.

Where this was taught

  • This unit Year 3 Lesson 1: What's in your feed?
  • This unit Year 3 Lesson 2: Is it always listening?
  • This unit Year 3 Lesson 3: AI that helps everyone

It also draws on Year 2 Lesson 4.

Remember When (slide 2) recalls Year 2 Lesson 4, Year 3 Lesson 1, Year 3 Lesson 2, Year 3 Lesson 5. These are retrieval questions: if your class has not had those lessons, swap them for a question about pupils' own experience. That does not replace any prerequisite above.

If your pupils haven't covered this

Teach Lessons 1 to 5 first. This lesson assesses them, so it is not a starting point for the unit.

What this lesson adds

Pupils apply the whole unit independently, which gives the evidence for the tracker.

The signature activity

Teams redesign a children's video app: a "why am I seeing this?" button, healthy limits, clear AI labels, no personal data. They pitch their design to the class.

The slides

Teacher notes and answers are in the lesson plan, and printable resources are at the end of the deck.

What to look for

Exit ticket: 1. Name two features that make a video app good for children. 2. Your why button says "Because you liked it." Is that enough? What should it say? 3. What did your app NOT collect, and why?

Look for pupils who explain each feature in terms of data, choice, honesty or privacy, and who give feedback that is specific and kind.

This evidence feeds the tracker for All progression strands: How AI works; Staying in charge; Safe, fair and honest; Learning Well; Design and create, and the unit ends with the IDEAL Challenge. Answers are in the plan.
