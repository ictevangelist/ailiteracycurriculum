Year 3: AI in Everyday Life

Big question: Where is AI in my life, and is it helping?

By the end of this unit, pupils can explain how a feed and a voice assistant use their data, say how AI can help people who see, hear or speak differently, ask for a hint rather than an answer, and say when AI has helped with their work.

Before this, in Year 2, pupils studied Using AI Wisely. Next, in Year 4, pupils move on to Machines That Talk.

Open the Year 3 folder in Google Drive

How this unit builds

Starting point. Pupils bring AI as a guessing machine, the AI Loop with a person checking, Bleep's Video Shelf, Keep to Myself, Try it first, helper or doer, and the IDEAL cue routine.

  1. Lesson 1: What's in your feed?
    Feeds learn from data and can make a bubble.
  2. Lesson 2: Is it always listening?
    Voice assistants record after the wake word; privacy includes others.
  3. Lesson 3: AI that helps everyone
    Guessing AI tools make things accessible, and people check them.
  4. Lesson 4: Hint, not answer
    Ask for a hint, not the answer.
  5. Lesson 5: Who made it?
    Say honestly when AI helped.
  6. Lesson 6: The Helpful Feed IDEAL Challenge
    Design a Helpful Feed using the whole unit.

Plan ahead. Lesson 3: Preview the optional demonstration tools beforehand.

Assessment. Teams design a video app with a why button, healthy limits, Made with AI labels and no personal information, then each pupil designs a learning feature as assessment evidence. How assessment works
