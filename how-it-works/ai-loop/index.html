The AI Loop: Learn, Spot, Guess, Check

How pupils understand AI that learns from examples (machine learning), the kind they meet most. It is a simple model: real systems are more complicated, and not every machine that makes a guess works this way. Every year group returns to it, so knowledge can build into a connected whole.

  1. 1LearnIt learns from lots of examples (data) that people choose or create.
  2. 2SpotIt finds patterns in those examples.
  3. 3GuessIt uses the patterns to make a prediction: a label, a next word, a video, a picture.
  4. 4CheckIt can be wrong without knowing. People check its guesses before they trust them.
StepWhat the AI doesHow the idea grows
LearnIt learns from lots of examples (data) that people choose or create.Year 1: it learns from lots of examples. Year 5: training data, test data, permission and fairness.
SpotIt finds patterns in those examples.Year 1: spotting a secret sorting rule. Year 4: patterns in words. Year 5: rules versus learning.
GuessIt uses the patterns to make a prediction: a label, a next word, a video, a picture.Year 1: it makes a guess. Year 4: next word prediction and how sure it is. Year 6: generated images, text and voices.
CheckIt can be wrong without knowing. People check its guesses before they trust them.Every year: people check its guesses, from 'is it right?' in Year 1 to 'who is missing?' in Year 6. That is where IDEAL comes in.
