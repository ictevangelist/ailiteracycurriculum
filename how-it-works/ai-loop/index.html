The AI Loop: Learn, Spot, Guess, Check

How pupils understand what AI actually does. Every year group returns to it, so knowledge builds into a secure schema rather than a collection of separate facts.

  1. 1LearnIt is given lots of examples (data), chosen by people.
  2. 2SpotIt finds patterns in those examples.
  3. 3GuessIt uses the patterns to make a prediction: a label, a next word, a video, a picture.
  4. 4CheckIt cannot check itself against the real world. People must.
StepWhat the AI doesHow the idea grows
LearnIt is given lots of examples (data), chosen by people.Year 1: it learns from lots of examples. Year 5: training data, test data, permission and fairness.
SpotIt finds patterns in those examples.Year 1: spotting a secret sorting rule. Year 4: patterns in words. Year 5: rules versus learning.
GuessIt uses the patterns to make a prediction: a label, a next word, a video, a picture.Year 1: it makes a guess. Year 4: next word prediction and how sure it is. Year 6: generated images, text and voices.
CheckIt cannot check itself against the real world. People must.Every year: Check is always the human's job, and that is where IDEAL comes in.
