How it works / Habits

Learning Well with AI

"Knowing how to use technology to support learning" is the most important outcome in any digital curriculum, and usually the vaguest. Here it has its own progression, as the taught form of the IDEAL L habit, drawing on cognitive science: when the machine does the thinking, the child learns less.

It is taught explicitly once in each phase, and a short Learning Well prompt appears in other lessons so it becomes a habit rather than a topic.

PhaseThe habitWhat pupils can do
KS1
Year 2 Lesson 3		Try it firstExplain that their brain grows when they do the hard thinking; use technology to practise and to show what they know, after they have had a go themselves.
Lower KS2
Year 3 Lesson 4		Hint, not answerTell the difference between help that makes them think (a hint, a question, feedback) and help that thinks for them (the answer); ask for the first kind.
Upper KS2
Year 5 Lesson 5		Plan, use, provePlan how AI could support a specific learning goal using STAIR (Specific, Tell, Actionable, Iterate, Role); use it to quiz themselves and to get feedback on a draft, deciding what to act on; then prove the learning is theirs by explaining it without the tool.
